# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-27

---

### 1. Introduction

Our Family Story (the "App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information appropriately. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it may be shared with third parties.

---

### 2. Information We Collect

#### 2-1. Information you provide
- Text content of posts
- Images attached to posts
- Categories (freely created tags)
- Family name and invite codes
- Kid profile display names and avatar images (entered by family members)

#### 2-2. Authentication information
- Authentication information provided when you sign in with Google, Apple, or as a guest (anonymous), such as email address or name, as provided by each sign-in method
- If you sign in with Apple, we store an encrypted copy of the refresh token issued by Apple, used solely to revoke access on Apple's side when you delete your account

#### 2-3. Automatically collected information
- Push notification tokens used to deliver notifications
- Device platform (iOS / Android)
- Language setting used for notification text

---

### 3. How We Use Information

| Purpose | Data used |
|---|---|
| Providing app features (saving posts, sharing within the family, search) | Post text, images, categories, family information |
| Notifying family members of new posts | Notification token, poster's name, part of the post text |
| Revoking access on Apple's side when an account is deleted | Encrypted refresh token |
| Improving the app and investigating issues | Usage of the data above |

---

### 4. Third-Party Data Sharing

The App uses the following third-party services, each of which processes data according to its own privacy policy.

#### Supabase (database, authentication, real-time sync)
- Used to store posts, family information, categories, and authentication data, and to sync data in real time across family members.
- Data is stored on Supabase's servers (AWS).
- [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

#### Cloudflare (image storage)
- Images attached to posts and avatar images are stored on Cloudflare R2.
- [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

#### Expo (push notification delivery)
- Used to deliver notifications about new posts to family members' devices.
- The content sent includes the poster's name and a preview of the post text.
- [Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

#### Google (Google Sign-In)
- Used only for authentication with your Google account.
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

#### Apple (Sign in with Apple)
- Used for authentication with your Apple account, and to revoke access when you delete your account.

The App does not use any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or AI services that receive your data.

---

### 5. About Kid Profiles

The "kid profile" feature does not create an account for the child. Instead, a family member (such as a parent or guardian) enters and manages a display name and avatar image on the child's behalf, and can post on the child's behalf.

- Kid profiles do not have their own authentication credentials, and the App does not provide any means for the child to sign in or give consent directly.
- Entering, changing, or deleting kid profile information is the responsibility of the family account holder (parent or guardian) who manages it.

---

### 6. Data Retention

- Posts, images, and family information are retained until deleted by the user, or until all family members have deleted their accounts.
- If you delete your account, authentication-related information (such as the encrypted refresh token) is deleted. However, because posts are designed to remain as a shared family record, only the identifiers linking the deleted account to its actions are anonymized — the post content itself remains in the family timeline.
